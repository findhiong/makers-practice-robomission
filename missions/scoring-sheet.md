## Competition rules worth practising now

- 👆 **One press to start.** Your code must do its own waiting. That's what `clean_start()` is for.
- 📏 **No adjusting at the table.** Measure black and white during practice, and put the numbers in your code.
- 🌊 **Expect a messy table.** Real mats are wavy and the lights change. Gyro steering and lines help more than counting rotations.
- 🗣️ **Explain your code.** Judges can ask either of you what any line does.
