# Mission 8: The Full Run 🏆

**Your goal:** Score as many points as you can in **2 minutes**, just like competition day.

**Maximum:** 255 points ⭐

---

## Step 1: Pick your missions 🎯

You probably can't do everything in 2 minutes. Look at the [scoring sheet](scoring-sheet.md) and decide together:

- Which missions are worth the most points?
- Which are closest to where the robot already is?
- Which can you do **reliably**?

Write your order here:

1. ____________________
2. ____________________
3. ____________________

> 💡 A mission that works 9 times out of 10 is better than a faster one that works half the time.

## Step 2: Build it in pieces 🧩

1. Open [mission-8.py](code/mission-8.py).
2. Tap the **download** button (the arrow pointing down, near the top right of the code), then **Download**.
3. In GearsBot, tap **File > Load Python from your computer**.
4. Find **mission-8.py** (it's in **Downloads**) and tap it.
5. Tap the **Python** tab to see the code.

There's one empty block for each mission:

```python
def cables():
    pass
```

Replace `pass` with your code for that mission. Get **one** working before starting the next. Then put them in your order at the bottom.

## Step 3: Score it ✍️

The timer stops the robot after 2 minutes. Score the run with the [scoring sheet](scoring-sheet.md).

## Step 4: Level up 🔀

When you're ready, go to the [start page](../README.md) and tap **🔀 Open the field with shuffled notes**. Now four notes are shuffled each time, so your robot must use `front_sensor.color()` to find out which note is which.
